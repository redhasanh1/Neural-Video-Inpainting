AI Background Removal for Videos

MarkRemoverAI's background remover lets you replace, blur, or remove video backgrounds without a physical green screen. The AI separates the foreground subject from the background in every frame, giving you a clean transparent layer or a custom backdrop.

No Green Screen Required

Traditional background removal needs a controlled studio setup. Our AI works with regular footage, detecting people and objects automatically so you can replace the background later.

Transparent MP4 Export

Export your subject with a transparent background for use in editors like Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, or Canva. Perfect for overlays, intros, and motion graphics.

Background Blur and Replacement

Choose a solid color, upload a custom image, or apply a Gaussian blur to keep the focus on your subject while hiding a messy or distracting background.

Common Use Cases

  • Create professional-looking video calls and presentations without a green screen.
  • Remove tourists or pedestrians from travel footage by masking the background.
  • Blur faces, license plates, or sensitive information in the background.
  • Produce social-media content with branded or animated backdrops.
  • Prepare e-learning videos with a consistent, distraction-free background.
